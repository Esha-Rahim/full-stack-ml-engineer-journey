## Expected Outcome

This repository is intended to document the development of production-oriented ML and AI engineering capability.

The goal is to become capable of building, evaluating, deploying, monitoring, and improving machine-learning systems, followed by hands-on specialization in modern AI and LLM-based systems.

The final professional role depends on demonstrated skills, experience, and the requirements of a specific employer; this repository does not claim a guaranteed job title or seniority.
